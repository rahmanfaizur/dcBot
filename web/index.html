Commands

Everything you need in voice — calm, simple, one slash away.

  • /play Search, YouTube, Spotify, or a whole playlist.
  • /nowplaying Bring the player panel back to the bottom of chat.
  • /queue See what’s next. Remove tracks or bump one to play next.
  • /skip Skip now — or let the room Vote Skip together.
  • /pause /resume Take a breath. Continue when you’re ready.
  • Fun Fact A little trivia about the song that’s playing.
